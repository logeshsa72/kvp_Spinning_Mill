Production Process

From Fibre to Finished Yarn

Every stage of our production is engineered for consistency — from the blowroom to the final quality check before dispatch.

01

BlowRoom & Carding

TRUTZSCHLER LINES

Modern blowroom lines with auto-levellers clean and open raw cotton to precise micronaire values. High-production carding machines deliver consistent sliver with minimal nep count.

  • Auto-levelling blowroom
  • Nep reduction < 80/g
  • Consistent sliver weight
  • Dust extraction systems
02

Drawing & Simplex

LMW LINES

Precision draw frames with auto-levelling ensure tight CV% at every stage, delivering uniform sliver ready for high-speed spinning.

  • Fibre parallelization
  • Roving twist control
  • Tight CV% tolerances
03

Ring Spinning

15,000+ SPINDLES

High-speed ring frames running across a wide count range from Ne 33s to Ne 66s. Equipped with compact spinning attachments for low-hairiness, high-strength yarn demanded by premium fabric mills.

  • Ne 33s to Ne 66s range
  • Compact spinning option
  • Low hairiness H3 values
  • S & Z twist available
04

Silk Reeling

800+ ENDS

Dedicated silk reeling unit processing cocoons into raw silk filament with high production efficiency. Produces uniform denier silk yarn from 20/22D to 80/64D for luxury fabric applications.

  • 20/22D to 80/64D range
  • High reeling efficiency
  • Consistent denier control
  • Luxury fabric grade output
05

TFO Twisting

1,000+ SPINDLES

Two-for-one twisting machines for doubled and twisted yarn production. Delivers superior twist uniformity and strength for technical textiles, sewing thread, and industrial applications.

  • Doubled & twisted yarn
  • Superior twist uniformity
  • Industrial grade strength
  • Custom TPI on request
06

Quality Lab & Testing

100% LOT TESTING

In-house quality laboratory equipped with Uster Tester for evenness, imperfection index, and hairiness measurement. Every lot is tested before dispatch — no exceptions.

  • Uster evenness testing
  • IPI & hairiness reports
  • CSP & RKM measurement
  • Lot-wise test certificates
